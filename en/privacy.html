What stays on your device

Saved games, records and settings are stored only on your device. They never leave it and are deleted together with the app.

Game Center

Leaderboards run on Apple's Game Center. If you are signed in, the app submits your scores to Game Center. Other players see them there together with your Game Center name. We have no access to this data. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Purchases

You buy the full version through the App Store. Apple handles the payment. We learn neither your name nor your payment details.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Since we don't store any data about you, we can usually only confirm exactly that. Write to us at the address above.
