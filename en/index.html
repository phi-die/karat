KARAT

Match-3 with glass gems for iPhone. Swap gems, Points times Mult, jewelry that changes the rules, and a run that never ends.

Help

What's free? The Daily Run and the Masterpiece, new every day, and the run up to the end of act 2. The full version (one-time, no subscription) unlocks the endless run, Zen, Blitz and Magpie.

Full version gone after a new phone? Play > Full version > “Restore purchases”.

Got a code? Play > Full version > “Redeem code”.

No leaderboards? They run on Game Center. Sign in to Game Center in your iPhone's Settings.
